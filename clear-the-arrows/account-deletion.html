Request Account Deletion

Clear the Arrows: Tap Puzzle is developed and published by Rizu Apps. You can request deletion of your game account and the personal data associated with it.

You do not need to install, reopen or sign in to the game to submit a request through this page.

Request Account Deletion by Email

Support email: rizwanliaqat1@gmail.com

Email subject: Clear the Arrows - Account Deletion Request

If the button does not open your email application, copy the address above and send the request manually. This button opens an email draft; you must send the email to submit your request.

How to Submit a Request

  1. Send an email using the button or address above, preferably from the email address associated with your game account.
  2. Include the Google Sign-In email address used for Clear the Arrows and state that you want your account and associated data deleted.
  3. We may request reasonable proof of account ownership to protect players against unauthorized deletion.
  4. After verification, Rizu Apps will process the account and associated data deletion and communicate the outcome through the support email conversation.
Security: Never send your Google password, verification codes, recovery codes or other private credentials.

Account Deletion Inside the Game

Signed-in players can also use the account deletion option available inside Clear the Arrows. The email request method on this page remains available if you cannot access your account or device.

An in-app deletion request may create a record for processing. Submitting a request alone does not confirm that all associated records have been deleted. Contact support if you need to check the outcome.

What Data Is Deleted?

Verified account deletion covers the game account and its associated personal and gameplay data held by Rizu Apps, including the following categories where they exist:

  • Authentication and profile: the Firebase Authentication game account, account identifier, email address and associated profile information.
  • Campaign and rewards: cloud-saved level progress, coin and star balances, reward records, seven-day rewards and rewarded-video history.
  • Challenges and statistics: Daily challenge history, streaks, wins, achievements, first-try records and gameplay statistics.
  • Preferences and other account records: cloud-saved settings, theme preferences and associated Firestore documents and subcollections.
  • Ranking data: any leaderboard entries, ranking attempts or related personal records associated with your account, where present.

Only data that must legitimately be retained for the purposes explained below is excluded from deletion.

Permanent deletion: Account deletion removes your account-linked saved game data and rewards. This data cannot be restored after deletion. Your personal Google account and other Google services are not deleted.

Processing Time and Data Retention

We aim to process verified requests promptly and in accordance with applicable legal deadlines. We will communicate the expected processing timeframe, any necessary delay and the outcome through the support email conversation.

Sending a request does not mean deletion has already been completed. If you have not received a response or need an update, contact rizwanliaqat1@gmail.com using the same email conversation.

Limited information, such as records needed to document the deletion request, may be retained only where necessary for legal compliance, security or fraud prevention. Such records are retained only for as long as needed for the applicable purpose or required by law, not for continuing to provide gameplay services to the deleted account.

Information independently processed by service providers may be subject to their own retention requirements. See our Privacy Policy for details about third-party services.

Guest Mode and Locally Stored Data

Some game progress, preferences or settings may be stored locally on your Android device. Offline guest play does not require a Google Sign-In account.

Cloud account deletion does not necessarily erase local copies on devices that are offline or no longer connected to that account. You can remove local application data in Android Settings > Apps > Clear the Arrows > Storage > Clear data. Menu names may vary by device.

Warning: Clearing application data can also erase guest progress and unsynchronized rewards. It does not submit a cloud account deletion request. Do not clear data unless you intend to remove it.

Other Privacy Requests

If you wish to ask about your personal data, request removal of particular data while keeping your account, or raise a privacy concern, email rizwanliaqat1@gmail.com. We will review the request and explain the available options. Not every type of account-linked data can necessarily be removed independently of the account.
