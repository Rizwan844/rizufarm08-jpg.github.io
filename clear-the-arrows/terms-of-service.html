8. Guest mode and account switching

Guest mode allows play without signing in. Guest progress and preferences are stored locally. Clearing application data, uninstalling the Game or changing devices may remove local records.

Where supported, the Game may offer an optional copy of the highest unlocked level to guest progress when signing out, or an explicit guest-to-account transfer. These features are subject to their displayed conditions. Copying a level does not automatically transfer every coin, star, statistic or other account record. Declining an optional transfer keeps the relevant progress separate.

Check which account is active before playing or accepting a transfer. Account deletion is a separate action from switching accounts or signing out.

9. Saved progress, offline play and synchronization

The Game stores relevant progress and settings on your device. In versions supporting cloud synchronization, signed-in players may also save and retrieve account-linked levels, coins, stars, challenge history, rewards, achievements, statistics and settings through Firebase Cloud Firestore.

Normal gameplay may continue offline. Some updates remain pending until an internet connection becomes available and the Game can synchronize with the correct account. Synchronization is subject to successful authentication, service availability and supported record formats. Historical information that was never stored in the cloud may not be recoverable after clearing device data or changing devices.

We aim to support reliable saving, but network interruptions, storage failures, account changes and service outages may affect it. Reconnecting does not itself confirm that every save has completed. Avoid clearing application data or uninstalling while important progress is unsynchronized. Contact support if you experience missing progress or rewards.
