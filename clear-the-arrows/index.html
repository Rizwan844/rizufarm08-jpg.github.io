HOW TO PLAY

Every arrow has a direction.

Read the board, look for open paths and plan your next move.

  1. Find an open path

    Look at the direction of each arrow and check whether its way out is clear.

  2. Tap to clear

    Choose an arrow with a clear route. Its removal can open the way for other arrows.

  3. Clear the board

    Keep choosing the right order until the puzzle is solved.
