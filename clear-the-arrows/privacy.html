1. About this policy

Clear the Arrows: Tap Puzzle (the “Game”) is an Android puzzle game published by Rizu Apps, an independent developer (“we”, “us”, “our”). This policy describes how we and the services used by the Game handle personal information, including when you play as a guest, sign in, use online features, watch advertisements or contact support.

Features and data processing can differ between app versions, device settings and regions. References to a feature that is not enabled in your version do not mean that feature is currently collecting data.

2. Information we handle

The information processed depends on the features you use. The following categories describe data handled by the Game or its service providers where applicable.

Account and profile information

  • Firebase user identifier (UID), authentication/session information and sign-in provider.
  • Google-provided name, email address and profile photo URL when supplied through sign-in.

Game activity and saved records

  • Unlocked campaign levels, puzzle completions, Daily challenge history and streaks, wins, completion times, perfect and first-try outcomes and achievements.
  • Coin and star balances, life and reward activity, seven-day rewards, rewarded-video history and records used to avoid duplicate reward credits.
  • Game preferences, themes, sound, music and vibration settings, profile decorations and supported ranking records.
  • Pending save records, reward and completion identifiers and synchronization status used to reconcile offline or repeated operations.

Technical, advertising and support information

  • Network addresses, device or app identifiers, authentication logs, app and SDK performance information and security signals processed by the relevant services.
  • Advertising identifiers, approximate location inferred from network addresses, advertisement interactions and consent choices where advertising services are used.
  • Email address, request details and correspondence that you provide when contacting support or exercising privacy rights.

We do not ask for your Google password or payment card details through the Game. Do not send passwords, verification codes or recovery codes to support.

3. Google Sign-In and Firebase

Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication help identify and authenticate signed-in players. The Game uses account identifiers and available profile details to associate saved progress with the correct account and display supported profile information.

Cloud Firestore stores account-linked game records for supported online features. Firebase may also process technical information required to authenticate requests, maintain service reliability and prevent abuse. Authentication credentials and service tokens are used to establish and protect authenticated sessions; we do not receive your Google password.

Signing in is optional for guest play. Some account, synchronization and online features require authentication and internet access.

For provider information, see the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security.

4. Guest mode and local storage

Guest mode stores relevant progress and preferences on your device without requiring Google Sign-In. The Game uses application storage, including localStorage and, in supported versions, IndexedDB, for local saves and account-related records. Signed-in players may also have locally cached progress, settings and pending saves.

Where supported, signing out offers an optional copy of the highest unlocked level to guest progress. Declining leaves guest progress unchanged. Accepting does not lower an existing higher guest level and does not automatically copy every account balance or statistic. Other supported guest-to-account transfers are subject to the choices presented in the Game.

Guest and signed-in records are intended to remain separate except when you choose a supported transfer. Removing local data, uninstalling or changing devices can cause loss of guest progress, unsynchronized rewards or device-only history. Signing out or copying a level does not delete the original cloud account.

5. Cloud synchronization and offline play

In versions with expanded cloud saving, signed-in account records can include campaign progress, reward balances and reward history, Daily challenges, achievements, statistics, first-try results and supported preferences. The Game uses account-linked identifiers and completion or reward records to reconcile saves and reduce duplicate credits.

When the Game is offline, supported activity can be stored locally and queued for later synchronization. Reconnection may trigger an upload or retrieval of pending records. A connection indicator alone does not guarantee that all saves have completed. The availability of cloud recovery depends on successful synchronization and the app version.

Some older versions saved only certain categories to the cloud. Historical local statistics or rewards that were never uploaded may not be recoverable on another device. Some device-specific preferences and unfinished attempts may remain local.

6. Profiles, photos and leaderboards

Your own player profile may show a Google-provided display name and profile image, together with decorative frames. Displaying a Google-provided image does not itself require access to your device camera.

Daily and weekly ranking interfaces may be visible before online ranking services are enabled. A “Coming Soon” message does not indicate that your score is being publicly ranked. If online rankings become available, they may display a player name, rank, score and, where implemented, an avatar or decorative frame to other players. Account email addresses are not intended to be public ranking fields.

Ranking features, where enabled, may process challenge results, attempt identifiers, timing, integrity or reward verification information to calculate rankings and prevent misuse. Avoid including private information in any public-facing display name.

7. Advertising and rewarded videos

Versions of the Game that use Google AdMob may display banners, interstitial advertisements and optional rewarded videos. Rewarded videos may provide coins, extra lives or other in-game benefits when the relevant conditions are met. Reward history can be associated with saved game progress.

When the Google Mobile Ads SDK is active, Google may collect and share network addresses (which may indicate general location), device and account-related identifiers including the Android advertising ID when available, app and advertisement interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. This information supports ad delivery, measurement, analytics and abuse prevention.

Ad availability can depend on connectivity, consent, region, inventory and the app version. Advertising partners may process data according to their own policies. See Google Advertising Technologies and the Google Privacy Policy for further information.

8. Advertising privacy and consent

Versions using Google AdMob may use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present privacy and consent messages where applicable. Where available, the in-game Ad Privacy Options control allows eligible players to revisit those choices.

Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily remove all advertisements or all processing needed for security, delivery or measurement. Advertising may instead be limited or unavailable depending on the applicable settings and requirements.

Depending on your Android version, device settings may allow you to reset or delete the advertising identifier and manage other advertising privacy preferences. You can also manage applicable preferences in your Google account. Contact us if you cannot access a privacy choice that should be available.

9. How and why information is used

  • Provide gameplay, authenticate accounts and keep player records associated with the correct account.
  • Store, retrieve and synchronize progress, reward balances, preferences and supported statistics.
  • Operate supported profile and leaderboard features and credit earned rewards without duplication.
  • Deliver and measure advertisements, where used, and respect applicable privacy choices.
  • Protect accounts and services, investigate errors and misuse, and maintain reliability.
  • Respond to support, privacy and deletion requests and meet legal obligations.

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, processing may be necessary to provide requested services, pursue legitimate interests such as security and reliability, comply with legal duties, or rely on consent where required. You may withdraw consent through available controls or by contacting us; this does not invalidate processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

11. Security and international processing

We use authenticated access, Firebase security rules and secure connections to help protect account-linked information. Access controls are intended to restrict access to private saved records to the appropriate account and authorized service operations. No security measure guarantees that information is completely secure.

Our service providers may process information on infrastructure in countries other than your own. Applicable provider terms and legal safeguards govern those transfers where required. Local data may be accessible to a person with access to your unlocked device or backups.

12. Data retention

  • Cloud account and game records: retained while needed to provide account and game services, unless removed in response to a verified deletion request or other applicable requirement.
  • Local saves and pending records: retained on the device until replaced, cleared or removed by app or device storage operations; unresolved offline records can remain pending.
  • Support and deletion correspondence: retained as needed to respond to requests and document their outcome.
  • Limited exceptions: information necessary for legal obligations, security, fraud prevention or documenting a deletion may be retained only for the relevant purpose and applicable requirements.

Service providers may maintain logs, backups or other records according to their own retention policies. Retention depends on the category, purpose and applicable obligations; there is not one uniform retention period for all information. Contact us for details relevant to your data.

13. Account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your game account and its associated personal and saved game data through the account deletion option inside the Game, or without installing or signing in to the Game using our dedicated webpage or support email.

Request account deletion

Alternatively, email rizwanliaqat1@gmail.com with the subject “Clear the Arrows - Account Deletion Request” and identify the Google Sign-In email address associated with your game account. We may verify ownership before processing. Never send passwords or verification codes.

Verified account deletion covers the Firebase Authentication game account and associated personal records held by us, including account-linked profile details, cloud progress, wallet and rewards, challenge history, statistics, achievements, settings, associated ranking records and related Firestore documents and subcollections, where present.

We aim to process verified requests promptly within applicable legal deadlines and communicate the expected timeframe and outcome through the support conversation. Submitting a request is not confirmation of completed deletion. Limited records may be retained only for the purposes in Section 12.

Deleting your game account does not delete your personal Google account. Cloud deletion may not remove copies on offline devices. Clearing Android app data can remove guest progress and unsynchronized records but does not itself delete your cloud account. Account deletion is intended to be permanent.
